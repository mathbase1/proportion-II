How the next question is chosen

Direct proportion, indirect proportion and best buys are tracked separately. Each begins with a short question. Accepted answers move the learner along that strand’s sequence; after two questions the activity normally mixes in another strand.

An incorrect check closes that attempt. The next question uses the same structure and calculator setting with fresh numbers. Repeated difficulty reduces the arithmetic where possible. An accepted recovery question leads to the next related demand before the next strand switch.

Help is recorded separately from independent work. Exact and tolerance-only acceptances both count as accepted. No response-speed penalty is used. These rules are teaching choices, not a validated ability estimate or examination grade. This view shows this device only.

About this activity

This is an adaptation of the supplied Proportion Studio. It keeps short word problems, question tables, worked examples, the working space, review and local progress tools. The bank has 31 question structures in 22 vocational contexts, with newly generated numbers.

Direct proportion includes scaling materials, quantities, prices, mass, volume and tables. Best buys include unit prices, free-pack offers, whole-pack orders and delivery charges.

Indirect proportion has only two changing quantities. Team size and time are compared for the same fixed job, at the same individual rate. Packing questions keep the total stock fixed. There are no tasks that change both workload and team size, and no mid-job changes.

Non-calculator numeric model answers are whole numbers. Calculator questions may use exact one- or two-decimal-place quantities or money. No recurring answers need to be rounded. A calculator is available only while a calculator question is on screen.

Marking: every numeric hundredth within ±1.00 of the model is accepted. The endpoints are included; model matches and tolerance-only answers are counted separately. Supplier choices must match exactly. This generous classroom convention can accept a nearby non-whole count or a different price; it is not examination or workplace marking.

One valid check closes a question. Incorrect attempts are followed by a fresh version of the same structure. Help uses a different example. Only closed, attempted questions show their solutions. Viewing results does not expose the answer to an unfinished question.

Scenarios and prices are invented teaching examples. Health and care contexts concern resources and administration, not medicines, treatment or staffing requirements. Construction, electrical, vehicle and public-service examples are not safety instructions or professional specifications.
